## 🎯 What Is Ultron?

Ultron is a powerful personal AI assistant that lives right on your Windows computer. Think of it as your own digital command center that can think, remember, automate tasks, and even connect to your Android phone. Whether you want to ask questions, control your PC with voice or text, or let AI handle repetitive tasks, Ultron makes it all possible without needing any technical skills.

### 🧠 AI Reasoning & Intelligence
Ultron uses advanced AI models to understand your questions and provide thoughtful, accurate answers. It's like having a brilliant friend who knows a little bit about everything and can help you solve problems, brainstorm ideas, or explain complex topics in simple terms.

### 🖥️ Windows Automation
Tired of doing the same things over and over? Ultron can automate repetitive tasks on your Windows computer. From organizing files to launching applications, you can tell Ultron what to do, and it handles the rest.

### 💾 Persistent Memory
Ultron remembers what you tell it. It keeps a personal database of your preferences, past conversations, and important information. This means you don't have to repeat yourself—Ultron already knows what you like and what you've discussed before.

### 🔧 PowerShell Tools
For those who want more control, Ultron includes built-in PowerShell capabilities. You can run commands, manage system settings, and perform advanced operations—all through simple conversations with Ultron.

### 🔍 Research Assistant
Need to find information quickly? Ultron can help you research topics, gather data, and summarize findings. It's perfect for students, professionals, or anyone who wants to learn something new.

### 📱 Android Integration
Ultron connects wirelessly to your Android device using ADB (Android Debug Bridge). This lets you control your phone from your computer—send messages, manage apps, take screenshots, and more, all without touching your phone.

Unlike cloud-based assistants, Ultron can run entirely on your computer. Your data stays private, and you can use Ultron even without an internet connection.

### ⚡ Built for Performance
Ultron is built with modern technology (Electron, React, TypeScript) and uses SQLite for fast, reliable data storage. It's optimized to run smoothly on Windows, including systems with NVIDIA graphics cards.

## 📖 How to Use Ultron

### 💬 Basic Chatting
Type your question in the chat box and press Enter. Ultron will respond in seconds. You can ask anything—from simple math problems to complex philosophical questions.

### 🗣️ Voice Commands (Optional)
If you have a microphone, you can use voice input. Click the microphone icon and speak your command. Ultron will transcribe and process it automatically.

### 📁 Automating Tasks
Tell Ultron what you want to automate. For example:
- "Organize my Downloads folder by file type"
- "Open my favorite websites every morning"
- "Create a backup of my Documents folder"

Ultron will walk you through setting up the automation the first time.

### 📱 Connecting Your Android Phone
To connect your Android device:
1. Enable Developer Options on your phone (Settings > About Phone > Tap "Build Number" 7 times)
2. Turn on USB Debugging (Settings > Developer Options)
3. Connect your phone via USB cable
4. Open Ultron and follow the on-screen instructions to pair your device

Once connected, you can control your phone from your computer.
